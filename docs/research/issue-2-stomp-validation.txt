Diagnosis
---------
An owner-driven, console-assisted fresh Isaac run captured Mom's actual boss
foot, not just a Broken Shovel foot. At game frame 142, source 45.10.0 was in
state 7, animation Stomp, animation frames 27 and 28. It generated two events:

1. amount=2.0, flags=16777216 (DAMAGE_CRUSH). The original pack cancelled it.
2. amount=2.0, flags=0. The original pack allowed this extra foot contact.

Host Hat was present, Pyromaniac absent, the pack global present, and the
original stomp callback was registered. Next-frame red health went from 6/6
to 4/6 (half-heart units): one full red heart lost across the sequence.
Both events refer to the same source and frame; do not count them as two
separately measured health losses.

Baseline seed: YXZ9 KTZH. Full preserved log SHA256:
91808a3be8fb811e75275468be8c930e1eac3495ee57956a02784c859f26751b

Change
------
Preserve existing DAMAGE_CRUSH immunity. Also cancel unflagged contact when
its source is Mom (type 45), foot variant 10, playing Stomp. Do not grant
immunity to other enemies, Mom's door variants, other animations, or other
non-crush flagged damage through this added check.

Keep Pyromaniac healing at the existing AddHearts(2), one full red heart on
the flagged crush impact. The extra contact events cancel without healing,
including continued contact in subsequent frames. Correct the obsolete
half-heart comment; do not change the implemented healing amount.

Cleanup and scope
-----------------
No diagnostic code is included in the repository fix or candidate package.
The known temporary issue2_probe.lua was moved out of the game root into
scratch after verifying its SHA256. A full game restart unloads its callbacks
still in memory. The original baseline package and log snapshots are retained.

The observed Mom-foot bypass is addressed by the narrow candidate. Other
stomp sources, champion animations, difficulty modes, and game versions are
not all covered. Do not describe every historical stomp report as verified.

Integration with current master
-------------------------------
Master subsequently added the stompImmunity configuration toggle. The PR
preserves its IsEnabled guard, so disabling the unnerf bypasses both crush
and extra-contact cancellation and healing. Two additional callback checks
cover the disabled setting independently for Host Hat and Pyromaniac, making
21 regression checks in the integrated version. The candidate hash and game
observations above refer to the earlier 19-check gameplay build, not a
gameplay validation of the configuration menu itself.
